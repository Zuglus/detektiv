Our agency was approached by a wealthy man. He had met a young woman fifteen years his junior, and it seemed to him that their romance was heading toward a serious step: he intended to formally propose to her.

On his friends’ advice, he decided first to check her life before they met — so as not to face any surprises later, which, as everyone knows, life tends to bring.

### Why Check Before Marriage

Marriage is not only about feelings; it also has legal consequences. Property acquired during a marriage generally becomes joint property, and one spouse’s obligations can affect the other. That is why, before taking such a serious step, many people want to know more about their future spouse than he or she has had time to tell — especially when the difference in age or status is noticeable.

Such a check usually answers simple questions: does the biography match what the person says; have they been married before and do they have children; are there any debts or court cases? In everyday life these things are hard to find out — and easy to keep quiet about.

### A Flawless Biography

Our detectives began by gathering and verifying basic information about the young woman. On the surface, everything was ordinary: born, went to school, entered university, well-off parents. Not a single detail of her biography raised suspicion.

### Still Waters Run Deep

But, as the saying goes, still waters run deep — and this case was one of those where the saying proved true. It turned out that while at university, the young woman had worked as an escort, hiding it even from her close friends. Not for long — about three months, as we established. But for our client, that was more than enough to end the relationship.

Episodes like this never make it into questionnaires or into the stories people tell about themselves — they are kept quiet for years. That is why, on the surface, her biography looked flawless.

### It Was Not About the Past

What decided things for him was not even that past itself, but the fact that she had lied. He was ready to turn a blind eye to the escort work and never bring it up again — his feelings for her were that strong. But he feared that she might lie to him again, once they were married.

> The past can be forgiven. A lie is dangerous because it can happen again.

### The Decision Is Yours

A check before marriage is not a sign of distrust but a chance to take a serious step with your eyes open. We do not pass judgment and do not decide for our clients: we establish the facts, and what to do with them is up to the client. One person will forgive and forget, another will not — and each will be right in their own way. In this story, our client made his decision before the wedding rather than after — and made it knowing the truth.
