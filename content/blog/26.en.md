Some assignments cannot be entrusted to just anyone: they require strict confidentiality, integrity and professionalism. Our detectives carry out such assignments in Moscow — within the law, protecting your interests and keeping your secrets. Sometimes a person simply has no one to entrust with a matter that outsiders should not know about.

### What Counts as a Delicate Assignment

The range of such tasks is enormous. It might be escorting a child to school on a one-off basis, at the request of regular clients. Or it might be accompanying especially valuable cargo from dispatch to arrival, including abroad. Delicate assignments also include:

- checking a spouse for infidelity;
- keeping an eye on teenagers;
- gathering information in archives;
- investigations;
- representing the client at negotiations;
- observing negotiations from the sidelines in order to tell the client afterward how it all went;
- matters the client has no time for: visits, collecting documents, verifying information.

### Why This Is a Job for a Detective

In a delicate matter, what counts is not only the task itself but how it is carried out. An outsider might let something slip, get flustered in an unusual situation or simply not know where the line of the law lies. A detective is used to working unnoticed, keeping his word and answering for the result. He thinks through in advance what could go wrong and knows how to act if circumstances change. And when an assignment involves people — negotiations, meetings, observation — it also takes the ability to carry oneself so that no one guesses whose interests one is acting in.

> A delicate assignment is carried out within the law, and the client’s secret stays a secret.

### Confidentiality

Everything the client tells the detective stays between them: the law expressly prohibits a private detective from disclosing the information he gathers. We do not discuss our clients, do not name names and do not talk about cases in a way that would allow anyone to be recognized. The stories on our website are told without names or identifying details as well.

### How Much It Costs

The price depends on the complexity of the assignment, its scope and the time it will take. All this is discussed at a personal meeting with the detective: he will listen to the task and give a preliminary estimate. We do not take on anything that goes beyond the law, and we say so honestly right away.

We carry out delicate assignments not only in Moscow but also in other Russian cities and abroad.
