“Detective agency” is a phrase that is becoming more and more a part of everyday life. But how hard is a detective’s bread, and who can become one at all? Let’s try to answer.

### The Path to the Profession

Let’s start with the law. In Russia, a private detective may be a person with a legal education, special training or work experience in police operational or investigative units. In practice, the profession attracts above all former police detectives and investigators: the basis of a detective’s work is investigation, and those who have done it know it best.

A future detective must have no problems with the law and no medical contraindications. He receives a license, which then has to be renewed, and registers with the tax authorities.

### Why Ask for a License

Hence a simple rule: before entrusting a detective with your case and your money, ask to see his license. A real detective will present all his documents right away. If you hear excuses instead of a license, that is a reason to be wary. A license number can be checked in the public register of licenses: it shows who the license was issued to and whether it is currently valid. Another document a real detective carries is his private detective ID.

### Investigation as a Craft

A detective’s work is in many ways like creative work: both the result and the fee depend on how an investigative operation is organized. That is why a client should not take part in the work himself. It goes against professional ethics and can sometimes even endanger the client’s own safety.

A detective’s range of tasks is wide: checking marital fidelity, searching for missing people and property, assisting in criminal cases and much more. A detective often has to be a psychologist as well — helping the client accept the truth that the investigation has uncovered.

> A detective helps not only to uncover the truth but also to accept it properly.

### Is It Worth It?

Contacting a private detective is certainly worth it. Detectives, like all people, differ. Pay attention to how they talk to you: a professional does not promise results before he has understood the case and does not hide the difficulties. And if a detective has all the documents, listens to you attentively and gets to the heart of the matter right away, it means he has traveled his difficult path to the profession for good reason — and you will really be helped.
