Two women came to us at once — a wife and her mother-in-law. Both were worried by the strange behavior of the husband and son: more and more often he stayed out after work until late in the evening. Yet there were no obvious signs of an affair — no smell of someone else’s perfume, no strange hairs on his clothes, no correspondence with another woman. There was no clarity, and the anxiety would not go away.

### Routes After Work

Our detectives began with the man’s habits and his routes after work. They checked the places he might go, his meetings with friends and colleagues, his activity on social media. Such work requires patience: a person’s routine has to be observed for more than a day or two to tell chance from pattern. At first nothing unusual turned up. But over time the picture became clear — and it was not at all what anyone had expected.

### Meetings Once a Week

The man was not simply working late or seeing friends. Once a week he met with another man. The meetings were personal: this man turned out to be his secret lover.

> The most unexpected secrets can hide behind an outwardly calm life.

### The Truth for Two

When we handed over the evidence we had gathered, both women were stunned. The mother-in-law had always considered her son an exemplary husband and could not believe what she heard. The wife had noticed oddities in his behavior but had not expected this. Once they had the truth, each of them was able to make the decision she considered right for herself.

### No Ready-Made Version

When people come to us with a suspicion, it is easy to start looking for confirmation of exactly that: he stays out late, so there must be another woman. We don’t work that way. Our detectives find out what is really going on rather than fitting the facts to the client’s version. In this case, that approach is what provided the answer: they were looking for a woman, but the truth turned out to be different. Our job is to establish what is happening, not to confirm someone’s fears.

### When There Are No Signs

This case shows that the absence of the usual signs of infidelity proves nothing. A double life may look quite different from how it is usually imagined. If the behavior of someone close to you has changed and the explanations don’t add up, a check will help you understand what is really behind the changes — whatever the truth turns out to be.
